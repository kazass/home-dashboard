# Home Dashboard 3.5

Selected direction: H4 Home Hub, with H3 weekly planning.

## Design system
Dark navy Cobalt is the first-run palette: #111721 background, #202936 surface, #f2f6fc text, #b1bdd0 secondary text, #a4baff accent. Inter and system sans-serif throughout, 16px body, 14px minimum control labels, 48px touch targets, 16px tile corners. Three columns on landscape tablet, two on portrait, one on narrow phones.

## Scope
Live time and next event; Kitchen, Garden, Housework, Trips, Sales and Notes areas; a separate week planner; meal selection and ingredient review; manual parcel records; five themes; display mode; original feature pages remain available. No fake account notifications, sample records, or scripted AI in the working app.

## Data and compatibility
The original v2 database remains separate. v3 records stay in home-dashboard-v3 and upgrade additively with the sales store. Backup version 3 adds sales; version 2 imports allow that newer store to be absent. Theme and tile settings remain in backed-up preferences. v3.0 source remains on codex/scalable-dashboard-v3; v3.5 is on codex/home-hub-v3.5.

## Later connections
ChatGPT actions, Vinted email notifications and shared phone/tablet sync require connected services and are not represented as live in 3.5.
